6-Stage Development

Product Development Process

Click each stage to view typical B2B turnaround timelines and deliverables.

Days 1–3

Concept & Tech Pack Review

Our pattern engineers review BOMs, construction callouts, and target FOB pricing to propose optimal fabric yields and seam constructions.

Days 4–12

Proto, Fit & Salesman Samples

Rapid proto sampling in 7–10 days followed by fit revisions, size-set samples, and pre-production (PP) seal samples.

Days 10–22

Material & Lab-Dip Approval

3-option Pantone TCX lab-dips, strike-offs for prints/embroidery, and bulk trim cards submitted for buyer sign-off.

Days 23–45

Bulk Cutting & Line Assembly

Automated spreading, CNC cutting, and modular sewing lines with daily output dashboards shared with your sourcing team.

Days 46–50

Wash, Steam & Private Labelling

Enzyme/ozone washes, tunnel pressing, hangtag application, and custom retail folding or hanger packing.

Days 51–54

Final AQL 2.5 & Container Loading

Final random inspection, metal detection, moisture-safe carton sealing, and complete export documentation.
