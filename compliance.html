Responsible Manufacturing. Assured Quality.

Transparent quality governance, worker welfare, chemical safety screening, and verifiable third-party audit readiness for international retail partners.

Quality Architecture

Multi-Stage Quality Management Systems

Quality is engineered into every workstation rather than inspected only at the end of the line.

Fabric Inspection

100% width, GSM, and 4-point defect scoring on backlit tables prior to cutting.

In-Line Inspection

7-point hourly roving checks at critical seam, collar, and placket operations.

Final Inspection

AQL 2.5 major / 4.0 minor random sampling plus 100% metal detection.

Product Testing

Colorfastness to wash/rubbing, dimensional stability, seam slippage, and pilling tests.

Digital Quality Assurance Systems

Lot-level defect tracking, root-cause corrective action logs (CAPA), and buyer-accessible pre-shipment inspection reports.

People First

Ethical Manufacturing & Workplace Standards

Our campuses maintain zero tolerance for forced or underage labor, backed by independent worker grievance channels and documented safety protocols.

Worker Safety

Fire-rated exits, automated sprinkler grids, needle guards, and certified first-aid clinics.

Fair Labour Practices

Written employment contracts, transparent overtime limits, and timely digital wage disbursements.

Workplace Standards

Ergonomic seating, climate-controlled ventilation, and calibrated task lighting across all floors.

Employee Welfare

Subsidized nutrition, maternal leave support, and annual occupational health screenings.

Standards & Alignment

International Standards & Compliance Frameworks

In keeping with transparent B2B reporting, we clearly distinguish between our internal quality/audit-ready protocols and external standards held by our partner mills or targeted for buyer-specific certification programs.

System Aligned

ISO 9001

International standard for Quality Management Systems (QMS). Our internal SOPs, CAPA logs, and document controls are structured around ISO 9001 principles.

Scope: Quality Management Alignment
Mill-Sourced Option

OEKO-TEX STANDARD 100

Tests textiles for harmful substances. We source OEKO-TEX certified fabrics, threads, and trims from partner mills on buyer request, with certificate verification.

Scope: Input Material Verification
Mill-Sourced Option

GOTS (Organic Textile)

Global Organic Textile Standard for organic fibers and ecological processing. Available through our GOTS-certified yarn and fabric mill network with transaction certificates.

Scope: Organic Fiber Traceability
Audit-Ready Framework

WRAP

Worldwide Responsible Accredited Production principles covering lawful, humane, and ethical manufacturing. Our facility policies align with WRAP's 12 Principles.

Scope: Social & Security Alignment
Audit-Ready Framework

amfori BSCI

Business Social Compliance Initiative code of conduct for supply chain working conditions. We welcome buyer-scheduled third-party BSCI audits at our campuses.

Scope: Open to Buyer Audit
Audit-Ready Framework

SMETA (Sedex)

Sedex Members Ethical Trade Audit covering labor standards, health & safety, environment, and business ethics. Documentation packs available for buyer review.

Scope: 4-Pillar Audit Readiness
