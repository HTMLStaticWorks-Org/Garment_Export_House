Flexible Sourcing Models

CMT, FOB & Full-Package OEM

Choose the manufacturing engagement structure that aligns with your brand's internal merchandising and supply-chain setup.

Model 01

CMT (Cut, Make, Trim)

Ideal for brands nominating their own mill fabrics. We handle CAD markers, precision cutting, assembly, pressing, and export packing.

  • • MOQ: 500 pcs per colorway
  • • Lead Time: 25–30 days ex-factory
  • • Full inline QC & needle check
Model 02 • Most Popular

FOB (Free On Board)

End-to-end fabric and trim procurement, lab dips, bulk production, AQL 2.5 inspection, and customs clearance to your port of loading.

  • • MOQ: 500–1,000 pcs per style
  • • Lead Time: 35–42 days
  • • Single-invoice mill + garment costing
Model 03

Full-Package ODM / OEM

Turnkey seasonal collection development, trend fabric library access, private-label packaging, and CIF/DDP warehouse delivery.

  • • Dedicated merchandiser & 3D team
  • • Custom woven labels & RFID tags
  • • Door-to-DC freight consolidation
From Tech Pack to Port

4-Stage Production Workflow

Transparent milestone tracking from initial costing sheet to final container seal.

Tech Pack & Costing

Submit BOM and sketches; receive itemized FOB quotation and fabric swatch options within 48 hours.
