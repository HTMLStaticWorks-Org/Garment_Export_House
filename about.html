Building Trust Through Quality Manufacturing.

Established in 2004, VALERIS has grown from a specialist tailoring workshop into a multi-campus international apparel export house trusted by global retail groups.

Corporate Heritage

Craftsmanship Meets Industrial Discipline

We believe international garment sourcing should never force a compromise between artisanal fit, ethical accountability, and commercial scale.

Our Mission

To empower global fashion brands with precision-engineered apparel, transparent costing, and dependable on-time export delivery.

Our Vision

To set the international benchmark for low-waste, human-centric, and digitally traceable garment manufacturing.

Company Philosophy

Every stitch carries our buyer's brand equity. We treat your tech pack as an engineering blueprint and every shipment as a long-term partnership.

Two Decades of Growth

Our Manufacturing Journey

Explore key milestones in our expansion from a 40-machine atelier to a 42-country export partner.

Milestone 01

2004: Foundation of Craftsmanship

Launched with 40 skilled tailors specializing in structured European menswear shirting and tailored trousers, establishing our reputation for clean seam architecture.

Milestone 02

2011: Knitwear & Physical Testing Lab

Commissioned our dedicated flat-knit and circular-knit campus alongside an internal physical & colorfastness lab to cut sampling lead times in half.

Milestone 03

2018: Digital Patterning & Lean Lines

Deployed 3D virtual garment fitting, automated multi-ply cutting tables, and real-time barcode production tracking across 48 modular lines.

Milestone 04

2026: International B2B Export Leader

Serving 120+ international fashion brands, department stores, and corporate uniform groups with solar-assisted facilities and closed-loop water recycling.
