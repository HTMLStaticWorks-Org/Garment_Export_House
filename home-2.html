5-Gate Assurance

Our Quality Promise

Select any checkpoint below to inspect how VALERIS safeguards every garment prior to export release.

01. 4-Point Fabric Inspection

Every incoming dye lot is unrolled on backlit inspection tables. Defects, slubs, and width variations are scored under the international 4-point system before cutting approval.

02. Stitching Precision

Roving inline inspectors audit 7 critical construction points per hour per line, verifying stitch density (SPI), puckering control, and seam strength.

03. Finishing Standards

Garments undergo automated thread-suction trimming, calibrated steam pressing, and dimensional measurement verification against buyer graded spec sheets.

04. Packaging Quality

Moisture-barrier polybags, silica desiccants, recyclable 5-ply export cartons, and GS1-128 barcode verification ensure retail-floor readiness.

05. Final Product Inspection

Independent internal QA and buyer-nominated third-party auditors (SGS / Intertek / Bureau Veritas) execute final AQL 2.5 sampling prior to dispatch.
